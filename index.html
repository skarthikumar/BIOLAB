How answers work
Questions must be in English. The app searches the lab manuals stored on your phone and shows the matching part with the experiment and page. If the manuals do not cover a question, it replies "The answer is not specified in the manuals."

Voice questions use your phone's speech recognition. Without internet they work only if your phone has offline English speech installed.
